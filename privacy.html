Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Birthday Tracker (the “app”) handles information when you use it. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the person responsible for developing the app. If we change our practices, we will update this page and revise the date above. Please review it from time to time.

Birthday information stays on your device

Names, birthdays, and other details you enter about people are stored locally on your device. We do not use Firebase Analytics to store those birthday entries. Anyone who can access your unlocked device or its backups may also be able to access information stored there.

You can manage or remove birthday information using the app’s available controls. Removing the app may also remove locally stored app information, depending on your device settings and backups.

Firebase Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to help us understand app usage and improve the app. Analytics may process information such as app interactions and events, app and device identifiers, app version, operating system, and related technical information. The specific information depends on the Analytics SDK configuration and the events enabled in the app.

Birthday Tracker is intended to keep birthday entries on your device; we do not intentionally include names or birthday dates in Analytics events. Google processes information collected through Firebase under its own privacy terms. Learn more in Firebase’s privacy and security information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

How information is used and shared

We use locally stored birthday information to provide the app’s features. Analytics information is used to understand app usage and help maintain and improve Birthday Tracker.

We do not sell birthday information. Firebase Analytics is provided by Google, which processes Analytics information to provide the service under its applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not otherwise disclose birthday entries to third parties through the app.

Retention and deletion

Birthday information remains on your device until you remove it or remove the app, subject to your device’s backup and storage settings. Firebase Analytics data is retained and managed according to our Analytics configuration and Google’s applicable policies. Contact us if you have a privacy question or request related to the app.

Children’s privacy

Birthday Tracker is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and its backups.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when the current version was published. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
